Frequently asked questions

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

In the app, go to Settings › Premium › Manage subscription, or open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

I bought Premium but it isn't active.

Open Settings › Premium › See plans and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My water isn't showing up in Apple Health.

Turn on Sync with Apple Health in the app's Settings, then check that WaterTracker is allowed in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check Settings › Reminders in the app and make sure notifications for WaterTracker are allowed in Settings › Notifications. An active Focus can also silence reminders.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget, then search for WaterTracker.

Will my data move to a new iPhone?

Your data is stored only on your device. It moves to a new iPhone when you restore from an iCloud or computer backup, or use Quick Start.
