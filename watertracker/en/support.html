Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

All features are free on the first 2 days you add water. After that, adding water, reminders, badges, widgets, Apple Watch and integrations need Premium; your logs stay visible. Reinstalling the app does not restart the trial.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

In the app, open the Settings tab and tap Manage subscription, or go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

I bought Premium but it isn't active.

Open Settings › See plans and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I edit or delete a glass?

Tap a filled glass to change its amount or time, or tap Delete this glass. You can also touch and hold a filled glass until the glasses jiggle, tap the “−” on the one you want to remove, then tap Done. A deleted glass can be undone for a few seconds.

My water isn't showing up in Apple Health.

Turn on Sync with Apple Health in Settings › Integrations, then check that Unda is allowed in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

How do I sync between my iPhones?

Turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings › Integrations on each iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account. Sync starts the next time you open the app. iCloud sync is part of Premium.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check Settings › Notifications in the app and make sure notifications for Unda are allowed in Settings › Notifications. An active Focus can also silence reminders. If you haven't opened the app for a long time, open it once (or add water from a reminder) to schedule the coming days again.

How do I add the widget or the Control Center button?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and search for Unda. On iOS 18 or later you can also open Control Center, touch and hold an empty area, tap Add a Control and search for Unda.

Can I use Siri?

Yes. Say “Add water in Unda” or “How much water did I drink today in Unda”. You'll also find these actions in the Shortcuts app.

Is there an Apple Watch app?

Yes. The watch app installs together with Unda on your iPhone, or from the App Store on your watch. Water you add on the watch syncs to your iPhone; if the iPhone isn't nearby, it is sent once they reconnect.

Will my data move to a new iPhone?

If iCloud sync is on, your logs appear on the new iPhone once you sign in with the same Apple Account and turn sync on. Otherwise they move when you restore from an iCloud or computer backup, or use Quick Start.
