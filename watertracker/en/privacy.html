Unda Water Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Unda Water Tracker is an iOS and Apple Watch app that helps you track how much water you drink. This page explains what data the app uses, where it is stored and how.

Data we collect

None. Unda has no servers, accounts, analytics or advertising tools, and we never track you. Your data stays on your devices and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own iCloud account — we cannot see it. The only exception is when you choose to email us yourself (see below).

Data stored on your device

  • Water logs and settings (daily goal, glass size, unit, reminders, language, appearance, streaks and badges) are stored on your device and shared with the app's widgets, Control Center button and Live Activity. If you use the goal calculator, the sex, weight and activity level you enter are kept only on your device to suggest a goal.
  • Apple Health: If you grant permission, the water you log is written to Apple Health, and edits and deletions from the last 30 days are reflected there. If you turn on Raise goal on workout days, the app reads the duration of today's workouts to raise today's goal; it reads no other Health data. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing and is never shared. You can change access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.
  • Notifications: Reminders and summaries are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Apple Watch: Water you add on the watch and the summary from your iPhone sync directly between the two devices over Apple's watch connection — never through our servers.
  • Siri and Shortcuts: When you use Siri to add water or ask for today's total, your request is processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the app only receives the action and prepares the answer on your device.
  • Free trial: The number of trial days you have used is stored in your Keychain and synced with iCloud Keychain, so reinstalling the app does not restart the trial. It contains no personal data.
  • Diagnostics: Crash, hang and performance reports from Apple's MetricKit are kept on your device for 30 days. They contain technical data only and never leave your device unless you attach them to an email yourself (see below). If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple may also share such reports with us anonymously.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync is optional, off by default and part of Premium. If you turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings › Integrations, your water logs are synced through your own private iCloud database with your devices signed in to the same Apple Account. This data is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we have no access to it. You can turn sync off in the app at any time and delete the stored data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud.

Feedback by email

If you send feedback from Settings › Feedback, your email app opens with a ready message. The app version, iOS version, device model, language, plan and a one-line summary of recent diagnostics are added below your message; with Report a problem in Apple's Mail app, the two most recent diagnostic reports are attached as well. Your water logs are never included. You can see and remove all of this before sending. We use your email address and message only to reply and never share them.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We have no access to your payment information. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the data on your device. If iCloud sync was on, you can delete the synced data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Entries written to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from children — it does not collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
