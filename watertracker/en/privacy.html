Unda Water Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Unda Water Tracker is an iOS app that helps you track how much water you drink. This page explains what data the app uses and how.

Data we collect

None. Unda has no servers, accounts, analytics or advertising tools. Your personal data is never sent to us or to third parties, and it is never used to track you. The only exception is when you choose to email us yourself (see below).

Data stored on your device

  • Water logs and settings (daily goal, glass size, reminders, language, appearance, streaks and achievements) are stored only on your device and shared with the app's widgets and Live Activity.
  • Apple Health: If you grant permission, the water amounts you log are written to Apple Health. The app does not read any Health data. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing and is never shared. You can revoke access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.
  • Notifications: Water reminders are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Apple Watch: Water you add on the watch and the summary from your iPhone sync directly between the two devices over Apple's watch connection — never through our servers.

Feedback by email

If you send feedback from the app, your email app opens with a ready message. The app version, iOS version, device model, language and plan (Free/Premium) are added below your message; your water logs are not. You can see and edit this before sending. We use your email address and message only to reply and never share them. This is the only way information reaches us, and only when you choose to send it.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We have no access to your payment information. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Purchases are subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Because all data lives on your device, deleting the app removes it. Entries written to Apple Health can be deleted in the Health app.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from children — it does not collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
