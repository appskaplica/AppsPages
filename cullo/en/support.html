Frequently asked questions

What's included in the free plan?

Scanning, every category and the estimate of how much space you can free are free. On the free plan you can delete items one at a time, up to 50 MB in total; reinstalling the app does not reset this quota. Unlimited deletion, bulk selection and “delete all”, video shrinking and automatic best shot are part of Pro.

Is there a free trial?

The yearly plan includes a 3-day free trial. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the subscription starts automatically at the yearly price. Prices are shown in your local currency on the purchase screen in the app.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

In the app, open the Premium tab and tap Manage Subscription, or go to Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

I bought Pro but it isn't active.

Tap Restore Purchases in the Premium tab. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy.

How can I get a refund?

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I deleted items but my free space didn't grow.

iOS keeps deleted items in “Recently Deleted” for 30 days; the space comes back once that album is emptied. In the Photos app, scroll down to Utilities, tap “Recently Deleted” and delete all. In the app, Premium › Empty Recently Deleted — guide walks you through it. Apple only allows you to empty this album yourself.

What happens if iCloud Photos is on?

An item you delete in Cullo is also removed from your iPad, Mac and iCloud.com signed in to the same Apple Account. With “Optimize iPhone Storage” on, some originals are only in iCloud; deleting them frees iCloud storage, not space on the iPhone. Cullo shows the two amounts separately.

Photos that aren't similar are in the same group.

Open the group and choose Not similar — hide this group; it stays hidden in later scans too. You can bring hidden groups back with Premium › Show hidden groups.

Some of my similar photos weren't found.

Similar photos are looked for among shots taken within a few minutes of each other; similar shots taken on different days aren't grouped. The Duplicates section also finds the same picture saved again later (re-shared or re-compressed), whatever its date. Originals stored only in iCloud aren't analyzed. If you've taken new photos, tap Rescan on the Overview tab.

How does video shrinking work?

Videos are re-encoded to HD (720p) in HEVC; HDR videos stay HDR. You compare the old and new version before anything is deleted; when you accept, the new version is saved with the original's date, location, favorite and albums, and the original goes to “Recently Deleted”. Slo-mo and Cinematic videos, and videos whose original is only in iCloud, aren't offered. Shrink all, one by one prepares every video and saves them with a single confirmation.

Only some of my photos are scanned.

If you chose “Limit Access” when granting permission, Cullo can only see the items you selected. To scan your whole library, choose “Full Access” in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Cullo.

How do I add the widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and choose Cullo. The widget shows your free space and how much you can clean; it's also available on the Lock Screen.

What is in “Blurry & Eyes Closed”?

Photos that aren't part of any group but are clearly out of focus or show closed eyes. Sharpness and open eyes are measured on your device. Nothing is preselected; touch and hold a photo to see it larger before you decide. You'll find it on the Overview tab and in the Photos tab.

How do I turn on the weekly reminder?

Turn on Weekly clean-up reminder in the Premium tab. It arrives every Sunday at 11:00 and you can turn it off any time. If it doesn't arrive, check Settings › Notifications › Cullo.
