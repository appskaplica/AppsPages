Cullo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

Cullo is an iOS app that finds duplicate and similar photos, bursts, screenshots and large videos on your iPhone and helps you free up space. This page explains what data the app uses, where it is stored and how.

Data we collect

None. Cullo has no servers, accounts, analytics or advertising tools, and we never track you. Your photos, videos and analysis results never leave your device — we cannot see them. The only exception is when you choose to email us yourself (see below).

Your photo library

  • Analysis: When you grant access, Cullo analyzes your photos and videos only on your iPhone: it finds exact duplicates with a file hash (SHA-256), and similar shots, sharpness, open eyes and face quality with Apple's Vision framework. Only files stored on the device are read for analysis; originals kept only in iCloud are not downloaded for it.
  • Viewing: When you open a photo full screen or play a video whose original is only in iCloud, iOS fetches it from Apple's servers. This is done by Apple, just as in the Photos app — never through our servers.
  • Deleting: Cullo never deletes anything on its own. Items you select are moved to “Recently Deleted” only after you confirm and iOS asks once more, and they stay there for 30 days. If iCloud Photos is on, the deletion applies to all your devices signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • Shrinking videos: Shrinking happens on your device. The new version is first prepared as a temporary file; if you accept it, it is saved to your library and the original is moved to “Recently Deleted”. Temporary files you don't accept are deleted.

Data stored on your device

  • Scan results: The groups from your last scan are stored in the app's own storage as photo identifiers only (no image content), so results appear without rescanning each time. File sizes and quality scores are also cached to speed things up.
  • Preferences: Groups you hide with “Not similar” and the list of videos that couldn't be shrunk are stored on your device.
  • Widget and statistics: A storage summary, the space you can clean and your cleaning statistics (numbers of items deleted and space recovered) are kept in the app's shared container so the widget can show them. These are numbers only — no photos or identifiers.
  • Free quota: How much you have deleted on the free plan (a single number) is stored in your device's Keychain, so reinstalling the app does not reset the quota. It contains no personal data and never leaves your device.

Contacting us by email

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and never share them.

Purchases

Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We have no access to your payment information. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the scan results, cache, preferences and statistics from your device. The number that records your used free quota stays in the Keychain so the quota isn't reset by reinstalling. You can turn off Cullo's access to your photo library at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from children — it does not collect personal data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
